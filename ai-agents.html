What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that uses a large language model to understand a goal, decide on the steps and carry them out using tools such as your calendar, CRM or phone system. Unlike a simple chatbot, an agent can take actions, like booking a meeting or updating a record, and knows when to pass a task to a person.

AI agents, chatbots and automation compared

The terms get used loosely, so here is how we use them. The right choice depends on whether the job is a conversation, a task or a fixed process.

Rule-based chatbotWorkflow automationAI agent
How it worksFollows a script or menuRuns fixed steps when triggeredUnderstands the request and decides the steps
Handles open questionsPoorlyNoYes, from your knowledge base
Takes actionsRarelyYes, predefined onesYes, chosen to suit each request

If your task has no conversation in it, such as processing invoices, AI automation is usually simpler and cheaper.

What AI agents do for Australian businesses

Phone calls

An AI receptionist answers every call with your business name, works out why the person is calling, answers common questions and books, moves or cancels appointments. It texts a confirmation and logs a summary in your CRM. Clinics, trades and professional services use it to stop losing work to missed calls and voicemail. For outbound work, such as appointment reminders or following up quotes, an AI voice agent makes the calls and records the outcome.

Website and messaging

An AI chatbot on your website answers questions from your own content, recommends the right service, captures enquiry details and books calls. The same agent can run on Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp or SMS. You can try a working example on this page: open “Ask Crenosoft AI” in the corner.

Internal tasks

Custom AI agents handle multi-step work inside the business: qualifying leads and booking sales calls, triaging a shared inbox, preparing quote drafts from job details, or answering staff questions about policies and procedures.

What an AI agent connects to

An agent is only as useful as the systems it can reach. We regularly connect agents to:

  • Calendars and booking systems: Google Calendar, Microsoft 365, Calendly, Cliniko and similar
  • CRMs: HubSpot, Zoho, GoHighLevel, Salesforce and Pipedrive
  • Phone and messaging: Australian phone numbers, SMS, WhatsApp and website chat
  • Business systems: Xero, MYOB, ServiceM8, job management tools and internal databases
  • Your knowledge: service pages, price lists, policies, FAQs and past email answers

Every connection has the narrowest permissions it needs. An agent that books appointments can see availability, not your whole calendar history.

How we keep AI agents accurate

The biggest risk with AI agents is confident wrong answers. We reduce it in five ways:

  1. Grounded answers. The agent answers from a knowledge base we build from your approved content, and says so when it doesn’t know.
  2. Guardrails. Clear rules on what it must never do, such as quote prices it hasn’t been given or give medical, legal or financial advice.
  3. Testing on real conversations. Before launch we run the agent through dozens of real past enquiries, including difficult ones, and fix what fails.
  4. Human handover. Complaints, emergencies, sensitive topics and low-confidence answers go straight to your team with the conversation so far.
  5. Monthly review. We read a sample of conversations each month, fix gaps in the knowledge base and report what changed.

This approach follows the “test and monitor” and “maintain human control” practices in the Australian Government’s Guidance for AI Adoption.

AI voice agents for Australian callers

Voice agents need local tuning. We configure Australian English voices, local phone numbers, Australian date and address formats, and business hours that follow your time zone and public holidays. Call recording and transcription notices are set up to suit the rules in your state, and callers are told they are speaking with an AI assistant.

Agents handle the common case well: booking, rescheduling, opening hours, directions, pricing you’ve approved and general questions. Anything outside that is transferred to a person, or a message is taken and sent to the right staff member straight away.

Privacy and data handling

AI agents talk to your customers, so they handle personal information. We design every agent around the Privacy Act 1988 and the OAIC’s guidance on privacy and commercially available AI products. Conversations are stored in your systems, retention periods are set with you, Australian hosting regions are used where available, and the agent only collects the details it needs to do its job.

How an AI agent project runs

  1. Free AI audit. We look at your call, chat or email volumes and where conversations get stuck.
  2. Conversation design. We map the questions people ask, the actions the agent can take and the handover rules.
  3. Build and connect. We build the knowledge base, connect your systems and set up the phone number or chat widget.
  4. Test with your team. Staff try to break it. We fix what they find.
  5. Launch and improve. Usually two to six weeks after kick-off, followed by monthly reviews and reports.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A traditional chatbot follows a script and can only answer questions it was set up for. An AI agent understands open questions, answers from your knowledge base and takes actions such as booking appointments or updating your CRM, then hands over to a person when needed.

How much does an AI agent cost in Australia?

Cost depends on the channels it covers, the systems it connects to and the volume of conversations. We give a fixed build quote in AUD after a free audit, plus a monthly fee for hosting, call or message usage, monitoring and improvements.

Will customers know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. We recommend telling callers and chat users they are speaking with an AI assistant, and always giving them a way to reach a person. This builds trust and follows Australian guidance on transparency.

Can an AI agent use our Australian phone number?

Yes. The agent can answer on your existing number through call forwarding, or on a new Australian number. You choose the hours it answers and when calls go to staff.

How long does it take to build an AI agent?

A focused AI receptionist or website chatbot usually goes live in two to three weeks. Custom agents that connect to several systems typically take four to six weeks, including testing with your team.
