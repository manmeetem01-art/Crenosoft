What is AI automation?

AI automation combines workflow automation with artificial intelligence. Traditional automation follows fixed rules, such as copying a form into a spreadsheet. AI automation adds steps that need judgement, such as reading an invoice, classifying an email or drafting a reply, so whole processes can run with a person reviewing only the exceptions.

What an AI automation agency does

An AI automation agency designs, builds and maintains automations for you. The work starts with a process, not a tool: we watch how a task is done today, count how often it happens and where mistakes creep in, then decide which steps a rule can handle, which need AI, and which should stay with a person.

The difference between traditional automation and AI automation matters when you choose a partner:

Traditional automationAI automation
HandlesStructured data with fixed rulesEmails, PDFs, calls and free text as well as structured data
ExampleCopy a web form into the CRMRead the enquiry, score the lead, write a personal reply and book a call
When inputs changeBreaks and needs rebuildingAdapts, and sends anything unclear to a person

Most good automations use both: rules for the predictable steps, AI for the judgement calls.

How we build automations that don’t break

Automations fail quietly when they are built quickly and left alone. Every Crenosoft automation includes:

  • Error handling. If a step fails, the workflow retries, then alerts the right person with the details.
  • Run logs. Every run is recorded, so you can see what happened to any invoice, lead or message.
  • Human approval points. Payments, customer-facing messages above a set risk level and anything the AI is unsure about wait for a person.
  • Test cases. We test on real past examples from your business before go-live, including the awkward ones.
  • Documentation. A one-page summary of what each automation does, what it touches and who owns it.

Measuring the return on AI automation

Payback is measured, not assumed. Before we build, we record how long the task takes today and how often it runs. Privacy and security

Automations often move personal information between systems, so privacy is designed in from the start. We follow the Privacy Act 1988 and the OAIC’s guidance on commercially available AI products: only the data each step needs is passed to AI models, credentials are stored securely, Australian hosting regions are used where available, and access is limited to named people in your team.

Frequently asked questions

What can AI automation do for a small business?

AI automation can take over repetitive tasks such as data entry, invoice processing, enquiry replies, appointment reminders and reporting. For a small team that often frees several hours per person each week, with a person still reviewing exceptions.

How long does it take to build an AI automation?

A single workflow automation usually goes live in one to two weeks, including testing on your real examples. Larger process automations across several systems typically take three to six weeks.

Which is better, n8n, Make or Zapier?

It depends on your needs. n8n suits businesses that want control over data and high volumes, Make suits teams who want a visual builder they can adjust, and Zapier suits simple automations across many apps. We recommend one after reviewing your systems.

Do I need to change my software to automate?

Usually not. We automate around the tools you already use, such as Xero, MYOB, HubSpot, Zoho, Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace. If a tool has an API or email access, it can usually be automated.

What happens if an automation makes a mistake?

Every automation has error alerts, run logs and approval points for risky actions. If something goes wrong, the right person is notified with the details, and we fix the cause as part of monthly support.
