What is AI consulting?

AI consulting is expert advice on where and how a business should use artificial intelligence. An AI consultant studies your processes, data and systems, identifies tasks AI can do reliably, estimates the payback, flags privacy and accuracy risks, and produces a practical roadmap. Crenosoft also builds and runs what it recommends.

Why Australian businesses hire an AI consultant

Most businesses already use AI in some form. Staff paste emails into ChatGPT, a vendor has switched on an AI feature, and someone in marketing is testing an image tool. What’s usually missing is a plan: which processes are worth automating, what it will cost, who is accountable and how customer data stays protected.

An AI consultant closes that gap. You should consider AI consulting when:

  • your team spends hours each week on repetitive admin, but nobody has measured where the time goes
  • you’ve been pitched several AI products and can’t tell which ones will deliver
  • staff are already using AI tools without clear rules about client information
  • a board, investor or major client has asked about your AI plans
  • a past automation project stalled and you want to understand why before spending again

What you get from an AI consulting engagement

Every engagement produces documents you can act on, not a slide deck of possibilities. A typical AI consulting project delivers:

  1. Workflow map. Each major process drawn step by step, with the tools used, the people involved and the time each step takes.
  2. Opportunity register. Every task AI could take on, scored and ranked (see the scoring model below).
  3. Data and systems review. Where your information lives, how clean it is, which systems have APIs and what needs fixing first.
  4. Privacy and risk notes. What personal information each idea touches and the controls it needs under the Privacy Act 1988.
  5. Costed roadmap. A 90-day plan with budgets in AUD, owners, milestones and success measures.
  6. AI usage policy. A short, plain-English policy telling staff which tools they may use and what they must never paste into them.

AI governance built on Australian guidance

Good AI consulting covers how AI is governed, not just what it does. Our recommendations follow the Australian Government’s Guidance for AI Adoption, which sets out six essential practices: decide who is accountable, understand impacts and plan accordingly, measure and manage risks, share essential information, test and monitor, and maintain human control.

For privacy, we work from the OAIC’s guidance on privacy and the use of commercially available AI products. It recommends that organisations avoid entering personal or sensitive information into publicly available AI tools, so our usage policies spell out exactly what staff can and cannot share. We also map each recommendation against Australia’s AI Ethics Principles.

From advice to working AI

A roadmap is only useful if it gets built. Because Crenosoft also delivers, the recommendations we make are ones we know how to implement. Once your plan is agreed, projects move into one of our delivery teams:

  • AI automation for workflows that move data between tools, such as invoice processing or lead follow-up
  • AI agents for conversations and tasks, including AI receptionists and website chatbots
  • AI development for custom apps, integrations and knowledge-base assistants

If you’d rather build in-house, we hand over the roadmap, specifications and vendor shortlist so your team can run with it.

How an AI consulting engagement runs

  1. Free AI audit (30 minutes). We learn about your business and confirm whether consulting is the right starting point.
  2. Discovery workshops. Interviews and process walk-throughs with the people who do the work, on site in Melbourne or by video anywhere in Australia.
  3. Analysis. We score every opportunity, review your data and systems, and draft the privacy and risk notes.
  4. Roadmap presentation. We walk your leadership team through the plan and adjust it with their input.
  5. Handover or build. You receive every document, and can start building with us or on your own.

Smaller businesses often complete the whole process in two to three weeks. Multi-department engagements usually take four to six.

Frequently asked questions

What does an AI consultant do?

An AI consultant studies how your business works, identifies the tasks AI can handle reliably, estimates the payback and risks, and produces a practical plan. At Crenosoft the same team can then build and run the AI systems in that plan.

How much does AI consulting cost in Australia?

Cost depends on the number of teams and processes in scope. We give a fixed quote in AUD after a free 30-minute audit, so you know the full price before any work starts.

Do we need clean data before we start AI consulting?

No. Reviewing your data is part of the engagement. We tell you which data issues block AI and which don’t, and include any clean-up work in the roadmap.

Will you recommend specific AI products?

Yes, when it helps. We are vendor-neutral and don’t earn commissions on software, so we recommend whatever fits your systems, budget and privacy needs, including tools you already own.

Can AI consulting be done remotely?

Yes. We run workshops in person around Melbourne and by video for clients elsewhere in Australia. Remote engagements follow the same process and produce the same documents.
