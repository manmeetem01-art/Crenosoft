What does an AI development company do?

An AI development company designs and builds software that uses artificial intelligence, usually large language models, to do work a standard app can’t: understand documents, answer questions, write drafts or make predictions. Crenosoft builds custom AI apps, connects AI models to existing systems and creates assistants that answer from a business’s own information.

When you need custom AI development

Many AI needs are met by existing tools plus good automation. Custom development makes sense when:

  • you want AI features inside your own product, so customers use them without leaving your app
  • staff need answers from thousands of internal documents, policies or past jobs, with sources they can check
  • off-the-shelf tools can’t meet your security, data residency or audit requirements
  • the process is core to how you compete, so owning the software matters
  • you’ve outgrown no-code tools on volume, cost or complexity

If you’re unsure whether to buy or build, our AI consulting team can compare both options with costs in AUD.

What we build

AI features in your product

Smart search, document summaries, drafting assistants, recommendations and data extraction built into your web or mobile app. We handle the model choice, prompt design, cost controls and the user interface, and we design for the moments when the AI is wrong, so users can correct it easily.

Knowledge-base assistants (RAG)

Retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG, lets an AI answer questions using your own documents. When a staff member asks “what’s our refund policy for custom orders?”, the system finds the relevant passages in your files and writes an answer with links to the sources. It suits policy manuals, product catalogues, technical documentation and past project files. See LLM and RAG development.

AI integrations

Connecting models to the systems you already run: an AI step in your CRM that writes call summaries, a tool that reads incoming purchase orders into your ERP, or an internal chat assistant that can look up customer records with proper permissions. Details on our AI integration page.

Internal AI tools

Purpose-built apps for one team: a quoting tool for estimators, a compliance checker for a case manager, a report writer for field staff. These often pay back fastest because they target a single, well-understood job.

How we make custom AI reliable

A demo that works on five examples often fails on the fiftieth. Our development process is built to catch that early:

  1. Evaluation set first. Before building, we collect real examples with correct answers. Every version of the system is scored against them.
  2. Grounding and citations. Answers are tied to your sources, so users can check them and the system can say “I don’t know”.
  3. Guardrails. Input and output checks block prohibited actions, personal information leaks and off-topic responses.
  4. Cost controls. Caching, model routing and limits keep monthly AI usage predictable.
  5. Monitoring. Live usage, accuracy samples and user feedback are reviewed after launch.

This mirrors the testing and monitoring practices in the Australian Government’s Guidance for AI Adoption.

Security, privacy and ownership

Custom AI often handles your most valuable information, so we design for security from day one:

  • Australian hosting regions by default, with data residency documented
  • Role-based access, so the AI only retrieves documents a user is allowed to see
  • Privacy design aligned with the Privacy Act 1988 and the OAIC’s AI product guidance
  • You own the source code, prompts, evaluation data and infrastructure accounts
  • Full handover documentation if you want to bring development in-house

How an AI development project runs

  1. Free AI audit. We confirm the problem, the users and whether custom development is the right answer.
  2. Discovery and prototype. Usually two to three weeks: requirements, evaluation set, architecture and a working prototype tested on your data.
  3. Fixed-scope build. Delivered in short sprints with a demo every fortnight.
  4. Launch. Staged rollout to a small group of users first, with monitoring in place.
  5. Support and improvement. Monthly reviews of accuracy, usage and cost, plus new features as you need them.

Frequently asked questions

How much does custom AI development cost in Australia?

Cost depends on scope, integrations and the level of accuracy testing required. We usually start with a short, fixed-price discovery and prototype phase, then quote the full build in AUD once the requirements are clear.

Do we own the AI software you build?

Yes. You own the source code, prompts, evaluation data and the cloud and AI provider accounts. We provide full documentation so your team or another developer can take it over.

Is our data used to train public AI models?

We configure AI provider accounts and settings so your data isn’t used for training where the provider offers that option, and we document each provider’s data terms for you before you sign off.

Can you host AI systems in Australia?

Yes. We use Australian cloud regions on AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud by default where the required service is available, and document any component that runs elsewhere.

What is RAG and do we need it?

RAG, or retrieval-augmented generation, lets an AI answer from your own documents and show its sources. You likely need it if staff or customers ask questions whose answers live in manuals, policies, product data or past project files.
