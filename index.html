FAQ

Questions Australian Businesses Ask Us

What does an AI agency do?

An AI agency finds the repetitive work in a business and replaces it with AI systems: agents that answer calls and messages, automations that move data between tools, and custom apps built on large language models. Crenosoft also runs and improves those systems after launch.

How much does an AI agent cost in Australia?

Cost depends on how many systems the agent connects to and how many conversations it handles. We quote a fixed price after a free 30-minute audit, then charge a monthly fee to host, monitor and improve the agent. All prices are in AUD and include GST where it applies.

How long does it take to build an AI automation?

A single workflow automation usually goes live in one to two weeks. A custom AI agent that connects to your CRM, calendar and phone system usually takes two to six weeks, including testing with your team.

Is AI safe to use with customer data under Australian privacy law?

Yes, when it is set up correctly. We design systems around the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles: we limit what data the AI sees, use Australian hosting regions where available, keep humans in the loop for sensitive decisions, and document how each system handles personal information. Our approach also aligns with Australia’s AI Ethics Principles.

Do you only work with Melbourne businesses?

No. Our team is based in Melbourne and we work with businesses across Australia, including Sydney, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide and regional areas. Most projects run remotely, with on-site workshops in Melbourne when they help. See our AI agency Melbourne page for local details.

Will AI replace my staff?

Our projects take repetitive tasks off your team, such as data entry, call screening and lead follow-up, so people spend their time on customers and decisions. Every system we build has a clear handover point to a person.
