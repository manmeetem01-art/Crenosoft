What does an AI agency in Melbourne do?

A Melbourne AI agency finds the repetitive work inside local businesses and replaces it with AI systems: agents that answer calls and enquiries, automations that move data between your software, and custom tools built on large language models. Crenosoft builds, hosts and improves these systems for businesses across Greater Melbourne and Victoria.

Why Melbourne businesses choose a local AI agency

Most AI tools are sold online with a free trial and a help centre. That works for simple jobs. It breaks down when the AI has to fit into how your business actually runs: your phone system, your practice software, your approval rules and the way your team talks to customers. A local AI agency can sit with the people doing the work, watch where the hours go and build around what it sees.

Working with a Melbourne team gives you three practical advantages:

  • Workshops in the room. Process mapping goes faster on a whiteboard than over email. We run discovery sessions at your office anywhere in Greater Melbourne.
  • Same time zone, same calendar. Support runs on AEST and AEDT, and launches are timed around Victorian public holidays and your busy seasons, not someone else’s.
  • Local context. We know the software Melbourne businesses use every day, from Xero and MYOB to Cliniko, ServiceM8 and HubSpot, and we know the rules that apply to them.

Victoria is also putting serious public effort into responsible AI. The Victorian Government’s AI Mission Statement sets out its plan to develop and adopt AI safely across the state. Our projects follow the same thinking: useful first, safe always.

AI automation in Melbourne

AI automation connects the tools you already pay for and lets AI handle the steps between them. Typical Melbourne projects include reading supplier invoices and creating draft bills in Xero, turning web enquiries into CRM records with a personal reply, and building the Monday management report automatically.

We build on platforms such as n8n, Make and Zapier, with AI steps added where judgement is needed. Every workflow is logged, has error alerts and sends anything unusual to a person for approval. Explore AI workflow automation and business process automation for more detail.

AI receptionists and AI agents for Melbourne businesses

Missed calls cost Melbourne clinics, tradies and agencies real work. An AI receptionist answers every call with your business name, answers common questions from your own information, books appointments into your calendar and texts a confirmation. Urgent or complex calls are transferred straight to your team.

Many businesses compare this with a traditional virtual receptionist service in Melbourne. The difference is that an AI receptionist works around the clock, handles several calls at once and logs every conversation in your CRM, while still handing over to a person when it should. Beyond phones, our AI agents handle website chat, email triage and multi-step tasks such as qualifying leads and booking sales calls.

How a Melbourne AI project runs

  1. Free AI audit. A 30-minute call or visit to map your busiest workflows and spot the two or three with the best payback.
  2. On-site discovery workshop. For larger projects we spend half a day with your team at your office, documenting each step, tool and exception.
  3. Blueprint and fixed quote. A written plan: what the AI does, which systems it connects to, how data is protected and the price in AUD.
  4. Build and test. Usually one to six weeks. Your staff test on real cases before anything reaches customers.
  5. Run and improve. We host, monitor and tune the system each month, with a plain-English report on hours saved and issues fixed.

Responsible AI that meets Australian and Victorian rules

Every system we build is designed around the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. We follow the OAIC’s guidance on privacy and commercially available AI products, which recommends against putting personal or sensitive information into public AI chatbots. For Victorian health providers we also account for the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic).

In practice that means the AI only sees the data it needs, we choose Australian cloud regions where the platform offers them, sensitive decisions stay with a person, and each system ships with a short document explaining what it does. Our approach also aligns with Australia’s AI Ethics Principles.

Frequently asked questions

Do you meet clients in person in Melbourne?

Yes. Our team is based in Melbourne and we run discovery workshops at client offices across Greater Melbourne, including Geelong and the Mornington Peninsula. Most build and support work then runs remotely, with regular video check-ins.

How much does it cost to hire an AI agency in Melbourne?

It depends on how many systems the AI connects to and how much work it handles. We give a fixed quote in AUD after a free 30-minute audit, then charge a monthly fee for hosting, monitoring and improvements. GST applies where relevant.

What is the difference between an AI receptionist and a virtual receptionist?

A virtual receptionist service uses people who answer calls on your behalf during set hours. An AI receptionist answers every call at any hour, handles several calls at once, books appointments directly and logs each call, then transfers to your team when a person is needed.

Can you work with the software my Melbourne business already uses?

Usually, yes. We regularly connect Xero, MYOB, HubSpot, Zoho, GoHighLevel, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Cliniko and ServiceM8. If a tool has an API or email access, we can usually automate around it.

How quickly will we see results?

A single automation or AI receptionist usually goes live within one to three weeks. Most Melbourne clients see time savings in the first month, and we report the hours saved each month so you can track the return.
